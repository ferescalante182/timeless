Concept demos

Nine fictitious businesses in Tijuana and San Diego. Each site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Carts, bookings, and forms stay in the browser and are never sent anywhere.

Taquería Mesa de Leña

A Calle Sexta taquería with a menu and a pickup cart that totals the order.

Claridad Dental

A Zona Río clinic with a visit booked by service, day, and time.

Lindero Realty

Homes in San Diego and Tijuana, filtered by price, beds, and area.

Club Senda

A Barrio Logan gym with a weekly class filter and membership comparison.

Meridiano Auto Works

An Otay shop whose repair estimate updates from the vehicle and the work.

Humo Lento Roasters

A Little Italy roaster with a product shelf, a cart, and a pickup checkout.

Albor Counsel

A binational practice with a four-step case-evaluation intake.

Atelier Nublado

A North Park studio where you book a stylist and a service.

Rumor Brewing

A Revolución taproom with a style filter and an events calendar.
